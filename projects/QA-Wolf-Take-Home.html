Overview

How QA Wolf Hires

QA Wolf’s hiring process consisted of three stages, progressing from an individual take home project to a paid technical assessment in a working environment.

  • Stage 1, Take Home Assignment: Complete the Hacker News Playwright automation task and submit the project.
  • Stage 2, Interview: Short interview discussing the submission, experience and approach to the work.
  • Stage 3, Paid Technical Assessment: Complete a technical task in QA Wolf’s own environment, designed to simulate how you would actually work on the job.

My Outcome

I progressed through all three stages and reached the paid final technical assessment, receiving A$220 for the evaluation. I was not ultimately selected for the role, but the process gave me a much clearer understanding of how I approach technical problems, communicate my reasoning, and respond to feedback.

What I Learned

Problem Solving

The process reinforced how much I enjoy breaking down unfamiliar problems, considering edge cases and validating assumptions.

Technical Communication

I learned that good technical work also needs clear communication. Explaining decisions, documenting reasoning and making code easy for others to understand are part of the work.

Seeking Feedback Earlier

One of the biggest lessons was not to wait for feedback. Asking for feedback earlier gives more opportunities to correct direction, learn faster and improve the final result.

The assessment did not end with an offer, but it changed how I approach both technical work and collaboration.
